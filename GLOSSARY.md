# Glossary

| Term | Plain-English meaning | Why a TPM cares |
| --- | --- | --- |
|Trunk-based development|Everyone merges small changes into main at least daily|Smaller batches mean lower release risk and faster feedback|
|Pull Request|A request to merge a branch, with review and automated checks|The main checkpoint for quality and the audit trail|
|Ruleset / branch protection|Server-enforced rules on a branch|Areal control, not just a policy document|
|CODEOWNERS|A file mapping code paths to required reviewers|Makes ownership and review routing explicit
|
